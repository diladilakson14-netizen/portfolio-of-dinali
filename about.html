About Me

Turning "what if" into interactive reality.

I'm Dinali Perera, a Brand Strategist and Interactive Designer based in Colombo. I began my design career in 2000.

Over the years, I have worked with brands in consumer tech, finance and lifestyle and partnered with established regional companies. By combining brand strategy, motion and interactive design, I make sure every part of a brand's story connects smoothly from the first concept to the live website.

Focus areas

Brand strategy and positioning

Defining brand identity, tone of voice, and market differentiation.

Interactive web experiences

Designing responsive, motion-driven websites and digital products.

UI/UX and design systems

Building scalable component libraries and user-centered interfaces.

Creative direction

Bridging visual storytelling with interactive technology for digital campaigns.

Journey

2018: Design career begins

Started working as a visual and web designer in Sri Lanka.

2021: Pivot to brand strategy

Expanded into holistic brand architecture and digital product design.

Interactive specialization

Focused on WebGL, micro-interactions, and high-craft digital experiences.

Today

Independent Brand Strategist & Interactive Designer partnering with global brands.
